🎯 Rules of Play

  • 1. Players take turns making a player guess one by one.
  • 2. Wrong Guess: A guess not on the board results in -5 points.
  • 3. Pass: You can pass your turn for no negatives (0 points).
  • 4. Hints: You can take a hint by revealing a category. You get a guess after the hint before turn moves on. Point reductions for hints are cumulative.

🏆 Points System

Correct Player Guess +10 pts
Hint Point Deductions (Cumulative):
  • • WC Runs / Highest Score: -3 pts each
  • • WC Span: -4 pts
  • • Team / Country: -5 pts
